How accurate is this?

Usually within about $10 of your paystub when your setup matches your stub.

It can be off by more on a check with:

  • A raise partway through the pay period
  • Back pay, adjustments or a bonus
  • A shift premium not included in your rate (2nd shift +$1.25, 3rd shift +$0.30)
  • Late in the year, after you reach the yearly 401(k) limit

Shift

Type times as numbers: 746 is 7:46, 437 is 4:37. AM or PM is worked out from your shift; type 1637 if it guesses wrong. Minutes convert to tenths of an hour the same way most timekeeping systems do (tenths of an hour).
